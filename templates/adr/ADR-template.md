## Context

What is the situation that forced a decision? Include the constraints, the prior state, and any specific incidents or feedback that brought this to the surface. Be specific — the reader in 12 months will not have the meeting in their head.

## Decision

State the decision in one paragraph. Use present tense ("We use X. We do not use Y."). Avoid hedging.

## Consequences

What becomes true as a result of this decision?

- **Positive.** What gets better.
- **Negative.** What gets worse or harder.
- **Neutral.** What changes that is neither obviously good nor bad.

## Alternatives considered

- **Name.**

A decision without alternatives is suspicious. If only one option was viable, say so explicitly and explain why.
